Use AfuChat’s public product pages as the source of truth for product names, positioning, and feature claims. The official lineup and feature labels can change independently from the portfolio, so older portfolio copy should not be treated as authoritative.

**Why:** The portfolio previously contained legacy product entries and capabilities that were not present on the current public AfuChat site.

**How to apply:** Before making future AfuChat product or feature claims, verify them at afuchat.com/products and the individual product pages.
